# Community Code of Conduct

LATINOS welcomes learners, teachers, dancers, designers, researchers, and developers at different levels.

We expect contributors to:

- critique ideas and movement descriptions without attacking people;
- state uncertainty and teaching lineage when relevant;
- respect body differences and avoid shame-based correction;
- avoid medical claims, guaranteed outcomes, or unsafe pressure to continue through pain;
- protect private class, teacher, and student information;
- credit sources and only contribute media they may legally share.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized comments about contributors' bodies, doxxing, plagiarism, and repeated unsafe advice are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the repository owner rather than posting personal evidence in a public issue.
